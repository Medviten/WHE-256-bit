# mod-worgoblin-high-elf
## F-133_mop-pandaria

### Features
- Pandaria (Mists of Pandaria's map 870, `HawaiiMainLand`) as a visitable continent, for the terrain in the squished client's `Patch-5.mpq`
- All 486 of its 5.4.8 areas (zones and subzones: zone text, `/who` locations, exploration), with English names from retail's AreaTable. Every area is flyable
- `.tele` targets for its ten zones: `.tele TheJadeForest`, `ValleyoftheFourWinds`, `ValeofEternalBlossoms`, `Kun-LaiSummit`, `TownlongSteppes`, `TheVeiledStair`, `KrasarangWilds`, `DreadWastes`, `IsleofGiants`, `TimelessIsle`
- MoP's world map for Pandaria: the continent and zone maps, explored-area overlays, zone highlights and the grid that makes zones clickable (`Interface\WorldMap\Pandaria.zmp`, built from the terrain with `tools/models/zmp_from_maps.py`). With it, MoP's World map, which shows Pandaria, and MoP's placement of the other continents on it, so they are clickable where that art draws them. The art is in WHE-common's `patch-MoP.MPQ` (shared with F-134), downloaded by `tools/models/mop_map_art.py`. Link or copy it into the client's Data folder tagged with the realm, e.g. `patch-MoP.256-bit.MPQ`: the client loads it only on that realm, so realms without F-133 keep Open Azeroth's World map
- Terrain only: no creatures, objects, quests, flight paths or graveyards

### Setup
- Client: `Patch-5.mpq` in `Data` (the squished client ships it), plus the rebuilt DBCs
- Server: map 870's `.map` files and a `870.vmtree` in the worldserver's DataDir. The map_extractor can't make them from the stock client, so build them on a PC with the ADTs extracted from `Patch-5.mpq` (`tools/models/mpq_unpack.py`):
  `python tools/models/swap_server_maps.py 870 <ADT_DIR>/World/maps/hawaiimainland <DATA_DIR> --new-map`
  where `<DATA_DIR>/dbc` holds `LiquidType.dbc`. Copy `maps/870*.map` and `vmaps/870.vmtree` to the server. No mmaps are needed (creatures there would only lack pathfinding)
- Keep it after F-010 in the manifest: Open Azeroth also has map 870, at expansion 4, which the core refuses to let a 3.3.5 account enter
- Not compatible with F-080_patch-reforged-m, which uses 39 of these area ids for Eastern Kingdoms mines

### Considerations
- Most of the art is missing: the tiles use about 90 Pandaria tileset textures, 1440 doodads and 300 WMOs that no 3.3.5 client has, so the ground is mostly untextured, with no trees or buildings. Adding them (from retail, converted) needs no change here
- The `870.vmtree` is empty, so nothing blocks line of sight. With the missing buildings that matches what is drawn; once the WMOs are in the client, the vmap tools can replace it
- Area bits are moved below 4096 (the 3.3.5 explored-zones limit) to bits no other modpak uses, as of when the files were generated
- NPCs greet, say farewell and get annoyed in their MoP voices (`dbc/[F-133]_pa_npc_*.sql`, as F-134's; files in WHE-common's patch-MoP.MPQ via `tools/races/zep_goblin_sounds.py --sql`). Other sounds, music and the minimap aren't included (their files aren't in the client either). The sky uses the Eastern Kingdoms' default light, since MoP's light bands aren't in the reference DBCs
- Generated by `tools/races/mop_map_port.py` (command line at the top of the script): change the script, not the SQL
- A stock zeppelin tower (OrcZeppelinHouse_Durotar01) by Honeydew Village in the Jade Forest, at (3200, -600, 232.3), for the regular Horde zeppelin: placed with `tools/models/adt_add_wmo.py` (uid 13900002, yaw 270) in tiles 33_25 and 33_26, which ship in WHE-common's `modpaks/F-133_mop-pandaria` (the realm patch, after patch-MoP-Pandaria.MPQ). Its Bladefist Bay counterpart (1270, -4915) is in Open Azeroth's tile (patch-OAMaps), since that tile holds Open Azeroth's other fixes
- MoP's faction intro movies for the first trip: movie 119 `mop_wra` (Varian, Alliance), 120 `mop_wrh` (Garrosh, Horde) and 121 `mop_jade_crash` (the gunship crash), in `dbc/[F-133]_movies.sql`. The files are WHE-common's `patch-A.MPQ/Interface/Cinematics/<name>_1024.avi` (the launcher's optional patch-WHE-cinematics.MPQ), re-encoded from MoP Classic's 1280 VP9 video and separate MP3 to the 1024 x 576 MPEG-4 the client plays. Not yet played by any quest; test with `.debug play movie 119`
- Hellscream's Fist, a gunship (Orgrim's Hammer's model), is planned for the first trip to Pandaria only: the player boards its lower deck from Bladefist Bay's watchtower beside the dock, and it is shown only while that quest is open (F-140). Its first-cut route (`tools/races/airship_routes.py hellscreams_fist`, taxi path 13900 in `dbc/[F-133]_hellscreams_fist_path.sql`) is kept for that, but the gunship itself is out of the world until then: running for everyone, it sat half under water at Bladefist Bay with its upper deck out of reach. The regular service will be a stock zeppelin from the east berth of Bladefist Bay's zeppelin tower (1270, -4915) to Honeydew's tower
