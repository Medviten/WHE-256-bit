# mod-worgoblin-high-elf
## F-052_mod-playable-qiraji

### Features
- Playable Qiraji (Horde), race ID set in config.sql
    - Model, textures and customization from Ilusixn's CoA 1.9.9 release (see Look)
    - Home: Orc (classes, start position, start outfits, action bars, stats, totems, druid forms, race-restricted
      quests and items)
    - Kin: Orc (voice, emotes and names, copied at build time)
    - Helmet prefix 'Ni'
- Generated by `tools/races/new_race_modpaks.py` (tentative, for testing)

### Requirements
- Custom client with `creatureSkinRaces` containing the race ID: the skin texture is composited whole and armour textures are not drawn on it
- Interface: a race button and the QIRAJI entries in `CharacterCreate.lua`, `GlueParent.lua` and `GlueStrings.lua`

### Look
Model, textures and customization from Ilusixn's CoA 1.9.9 release (CoA race 88), put in place by `tools/races/coa199_replace.py`. Author: the AllRaces v12 pack (Furioz, Corruption, Eunoia, Kalibros); see `CREDITS.md` in the release, which must ship with these files.

### Known Issues
- Tentative: not tested in game yet
- No racial mount
- Placeholder race icon, racial faction name and description
- Barber shop styles have generic names
- Character creation camera is not tuned for the model's size

### CoA Custom 1.9 racials
- Active: learned with the racial skill line
- From CoA race 123 (Qiraji (shelved in CoA 1.9)), renumbered; blocks at the end of `[BASE,F-052]_spell.sql`, `_skilllineability.sql` and `_spellicon.sql`
    - Chitin Plate: The hive hardens you. Reduces Nature and Fire damage taken by 3%.
    - Burrower: Sand parts before the swarm. Increases your movement speed by 3%.
    - Hive Reflexes: A thousand eyes see the blow. Increases your chance to dodge by 3%.
    - Swarm Frenzy: The hive drives you into a frenzy, increasing your attack power for 15 sec.
    - Wings of the Swarm: Your wings slow your fall and cleanse snares for 8 sec while hardening your carapace.
