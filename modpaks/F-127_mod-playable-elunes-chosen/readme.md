# mod-worgoblin-high-elf
## F-127_mod-playable-elunes-chosen

### Features
- Playable Elune's Chosen (Alliance), race ID set in config.sql
    - CoA's model and textures from Ilusixn's CoA 1.9.9 release (CoA race 118), prepared by `tools/models/coa199_race_prep.py`
    - Customization: CoA's own CharSections, hair and facial hair rows for its race 118
    - Home: NightElf (classes, start position, start outfits, action bars, stats, totems, druid forms, race-restricted
      quests and items)
    - Kin: NightElf (voice, emotes and names, copied at build time)
    - Helmet prefix 'Ni'
    - Model size and display scale from CoA's CreatureModelData/CreatureDisplayInfo rows
- Generated by `tools/races/coa199_race_modpaks.py` (tentative, for testing)

### Requirements
- Client files from `tools/models/coa199_race_prep.py` (in WHE-common's `patch-A.MPQ`)
- Interface: race button and the ELUNECHOSEN entries in `CharacterCreate.lua/.xml`, `GlueParent.lua` and `GlueStrings.lua`

### Credits
- Model and textures: the AllRaces v12 pack: Furioz, Corruption, Eunoia, Kalibros (STRHercules). See `CREDITS.md` in Ilusixn's CoA 1.9.9 release, which must ship with them

### Known Issues
- Tentative: not tested in game yet
- No racial mount
- Placeholder race icon
- Barber shop styles have generic names
- Character creation camera is not tuned for the model's size

### CoA Custom 1.9 racials
- Active: learned with the racial skill line
- From CoA (Ilusixn's CoA 1.9.9 release, CoA race 121 and the v12 racials of race 118) (Elune's Chosen), renumbered; blocks at the end of `[BASE,F-127]_spell.sql`, `_skilllineability.sql` and `_spellicon.sql`
    - Moonlit Ward: Elune turns aside the arcane. Reduces Arcane and Nature damage taken by 3%.
    - Starlit Stride: The moon lights your path. Increases your movement speed by 3%.
    - Veil of Elune: A ghostly glow slips blows aside. Increases your chance to dodge by 3%.
    - Moonlit Resolve: Elune clears your mind: removes all fear, charm and sleep effects and grants a brief immunity to them.
    - Elune's Embrace: The goddess mends your wounds, healing you over 15 sec.
    - Moonlit Veil: Elune cloaks you in moonlight: you turn stealthy and can keep moving (movement speed reduced by 30%) until you attack or are discovered. Can be used in combat. 3 minute cooldown.
